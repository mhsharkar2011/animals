Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our dogs and how to connect with us

Can I visit your dogs?

Absolutely! We love welcoming fellow dog lovers. Please contact us first to arrange a suitable time. Our dogs get excited about visitors!

Do you have puppies for adoption?

Currently we don't have puppies available for adoption, but we're happy to connect you with trusted local shelters and rescue organizations.

What do your dogs eat?

Our dogs enjoy a balanced diet of high-quality dry food, wet food, fresh vegetables, and occasional treats. We're happy to share recommendations!

Can I share my dog photos?

Yes, please! We love seeing other dogs. Tag us on Instagram or use #DogsLoverBD for a chance to be featured in our gallery.

How can I support your work?

The best support is spreading love for dogs! Follow us on social media, share our content, and advocate for animal welfare in your community.

What breeds are your dogs?

We have a mix of local breeds and rescues. Each of our six dogs has a unique story and personality that makes them special.
